# Firsts, DRAFT (candidate derivations; do not cite until adjudicated)

|---|---|---|

| First French expedition | Lapérouse's *Boussole* & *Astrolabe*, Monterey, Sept 1786 | ✅ solid (curated; C-A 15 livestock account) |

| **First whaler at an Alta port** | American whaling frigate ***Ontario*** (Capt. Alexander Bunker, Nantucket), **San Francisco, 13 Oct 1822** | ✅ **leaf-verified** (C-A 56 d195 leaf n189): *"la fragata Americana ballenera 'Ontario', procedente de Boston del Puerto de Nantucket… su capitán Alejandro Bunker."* ⚠ Excludes the **1806 O'Cain** at San Diego, Spanish officials logged it as a *ballenero* from Nantucket, but it was Winship's **otter**-hunting ship (the "whaler" label is a mis-description / cover), so it is the first vessel *called* a whaler at an Alta port, not the first genuine one. |
| First Mexican-flag (national) vessel | War-brig ***Morelos***, **Monterey, 28 July 1825** | ✅ **leaf-verified** (C-A 18 d123 leaf n155): *"Bergantín 'Morelos', Relación de los individuos que por órden superior son sentenciados á los presidios de California que conduce este buque transporte"* (the convict-soldier transport under the first Mexican governor's regime; among the presos, Joaquín Solís, future 1829 revolt leader). ⚠ Caveats: the naive winner *la hermosa mexicana* (1817) is **pre-independence** Spanish + a person-name FP; the *Asia* & *Constante* (May 1825) became Mexican by **capitulation** at Monterey, not by arriving under the flag; and the San Blas supply ships flew Mexican colors after California's 1822 adhesion but aren't yet flag-confirmed here, so an earlier genuine Mexican-flag hull (1822–24) may exist. |

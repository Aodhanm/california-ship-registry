1826-11-06 to 1827-01-05 · San Francisco
port-call alta exploration arrived

BEECHEY'S SURVEY: HMS Blossom (Capt. F. W. Beechey; naturalist Alexander Collie, Lt. Edward Belcher) anchored SF 6 Nov 1826, ran a careful hydrographic survey of San Francisco Bay, then Monterey (sailed 5 Jan 1827). The most evenly accurate published observations of any visitor to Mexican California to that date; nearly the sole source for Echeandia's partial-emancipation experiment.

  • Published primary source: F. W. Beechey, Narrative of a Voyage to the Pacific and Beering's Strait ... in H.M.S. Blossom ... 1825-28 (London: Colburn & Bentley, 1831), 2 vols.
  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California III
1827-10-29 to 1827-12-17 · Monterey
port-call alta exploration arrived

Second visit: Beechey's Blossom returned to Monterey 29 Oct - 17 Dec 1827 (then SF for water; sailed for San Blas 3 Jan 1828). The Arctic-support mission (rendezvous with Franklin/Parry) made California a wintering stop; the resulting books are a first-rank source.

  • Published primary source: Beechey, Narrative of a Voyage to the Pacific and Beering’s Strait (Colburn & Bentley, 1831), vol. II
  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California III

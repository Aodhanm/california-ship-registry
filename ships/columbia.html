1814-11-23 to 1814-12-21 · Monterey
port-call alta fur-trade / provisioning arrived

NWC schooner Columbia (Capt. Robson; Corney 1st officer) anchored Monterey 23 Nov 1814; landed barter goods by viceregal permission (answer of 16 Dec); 8 deserters; sailed for Bodega 21 Dec

  • Published primary source: Corney, Voyages in the Northern Pacific (1896), ch. III

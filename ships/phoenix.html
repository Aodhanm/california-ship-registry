English merchant ship *Phoenix* (Capt. Hugh Moore), from Bengal, touched Santa Bárbara Aug 1795 for supplies; left a 'Boston lad' ('un mozo Bostonés') who was sent to San Blas. [Ogden itinerary: Santa Bárbara 1795; merged v0410/v0411/v2123]

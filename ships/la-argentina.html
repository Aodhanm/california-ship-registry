1818-11-20 to 1818-11-27 · Monterey
port-call alta warship raid

THE ONLY FOREIGN ATTACK ON SPANISH CALIFORNIA: Hipolito Bouchard's insurgent frigate La Argentina (38-44 guns) with the corvette Santa Rosa (2nd = Peter Corney) sacked and burned Monterey 20-27 Nov 1818 under the flag of the United Provinces of the Rio de la Plata; then raided the Ortega rancho at Refugio (2 Dec), parleyed a prisoner-exchange at Santa Barbara (6-9 Dec), and touched San Juan Capistrano (14-16 Dec). Gov. Sola evacuated inland; Monterey rebuilt by spring 1819.

  • Published primary source: Peter Corney, Voyages in the Northern Pacific ... 1813 to 1818 (Honolulu: Thrum, 1896)
  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California II, ch. “Bouchard and the Insurgents”
  • Published secondary source: Peter Uhrowczik, The Burning of Monterey: The 1818 Attack ... by the Privateer Bouchard (Cyril Books, 2001)

Sources disagree: privateer (letters of marque), NOT pirate; flag = United Provinces of the Rio de la Plata (“Argentina” anachronistic). Monterey departure 26-27 Nov (Spanish) vs 1 Dec (Corney); Santa Barbara departure 9 vs 12 Dec; La Argentina guns 38 (Bancroft) vs 44 (Corney)

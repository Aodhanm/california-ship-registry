MALASPINA EXPEDITION AT MONTEREY, 13–25 Sept 1791: the corvette Descubierta (Alejandro Malaspina) — Spain's grand scientific circumnavigation, its answer to Cook and La Pérouse — did astronomy, botany and Ohlone ethnography with Fr. Lasuén, then ran a coastal survey from ~60°N down to Cabo San Lucas. Naturalist Tadeo Haenke and artist Tomás Suría were present; Née and Guío remained behind in Mexico. PREVIOUSLY MISSING FROM THE REGISTRY.

  • Published secondary source: Donald C. Cutter, Malaspina in California (San Francisco: John Howell–Books, 1960)
  • Published primary source: Malaspina, Viaje político-científico alrededor del mundo, ed. Pedro de Novo y Colson (Madrid, 1885)
  • Published primary source: Andrew David, F. Fernández-Armesto et al. (eds.), The Malaspina Expedition 1789–1794, Hakluyt Soc. 3rd ser. vol. II (2003)
  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California I:490–91

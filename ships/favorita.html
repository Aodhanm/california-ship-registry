1779-09-14 to 1779-10-30 · San Francisco
port-call alta exploration arrived

The Favorita (Juan Francisco de la Bodega y Quadra, 2nd-in-command; pilot Maurelle) entered San Francisco Bay 14 Sept 1779, a day before the Princesa, on the return from the Arteaga–Bodega Alaska expedition (reached 61°17′N, Spain's northernmost point); Bodega gave a bronze N.S. de los Remedios to the mission; departed 30 Oct 1779

  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California I:329
  • Published primary source: Maurelle, Relación del viage... de Arteaga, 1779 (MS; copy in the Online Archive of California)
  • Published secondary source: Freeman M. Tovell, At the Far Reaches of Empire: Bodega y Quadra (UBC Press, 2008)

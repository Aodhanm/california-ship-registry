Recorded visits

1792-10-09 to 1793-01-15 · Monterey
port-call alta exploration arrived

Bodega y Quadra (Spanish Nootka commissioner) arrived Monterey from the north 9 Oct 1792 aboard the frigate Activa (his broad pennant), wintered through the Vancouver season, and sailed with the combined fleet 14/15 Jan 1793. ACTIVA vs SATURNINA RESOLVED: his flagship = the Activa; the Saturnina was a separate survey schooner (the cannon Bodega offered San Francisco were to come on it).

  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California I:509–13
  • Published primary source: Bodega y Quadra, 1792 Nootka journal (via Tovell, At the Far Reaches of Empire)
  • Archives of California (BANC MSS C-A): C-A 54 i.119 (Sal → Arrillaga, 31 Oct 1792)

Sources disagree: departure 14 Jan (some sources) vs 15 Jan 1793 (Bancroft); deserter-orders MS dated 19 Jan

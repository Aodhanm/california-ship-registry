1788-09-17 to 1788-10-14 · Monterey
port-call alta exploration arrived

Returning from the 1788 reconnaissance of Russian posts in Alaska (Unalaska; met Potap Zaikov), Esteban José Martínez's Princesa lay at Monterey 17 Sept–14 Oct 1788 (the San Carlos under López de Haro went straight to San Blas); the intelligence on Russian expansion triggered the 1789 Spanish occupation of Nootka

  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California I:444
  • Primary archival source: AGI, ESTADO, 43, N.12 (PARES): expedition of Martínez & López de Haro in search of the Russian establishments
  • Published secondary source: Warren L. Cook, Flood Tide of Empire: Spain and the Pacific Northwest, 1543–1819 (Yale, 1973)

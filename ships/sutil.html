1792-09-22 to 1792-10-26 · Monterey
port-call alta exploration arrived

Galiano & Valdés's Sutil at Monterey 22 Sept–26 Oct 1792, returning from the LAST Spanish survey of the Strait of Juan de Fuca and the circumnavigation of Vancouver Island (alongside Vancouver); the Monterey stay was spent writing up the northern charts. Published as the Relación... Sutil y Mexicana (Madrid, 1802).

  • Published primary source: Relación del viage hecho por las goletas Sutil y Mexicana en el año de 1792 (Madrid: Imprenta Real, 1802)
  • Published primary source: John Kendrick (ed./trans.), The Voyage of Sutil and Mexicana, 1792 (Arthur H. Clark, 1991)
  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California I:506–07

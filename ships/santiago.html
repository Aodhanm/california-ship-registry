1774-05 to 1774-06 · Monterey
port-call alta exploration arrived

FIRST SPANISH VOYAGE UP THE NW COAST: Juan Pérez's Santiago (pilot Esteban José Martínez; diarists Crespí & Peña) touched San Diego then Monterey (spring 1774) before running north to ~54°40′–55°N off Haida Gwaii and the first European contact at Nootka; made NO landing or possession — the failure that prompted the 1775 follow-up

  • Published primary source: Herbert K. Beals, Juan Pérez on the Northwest Coast: Six Documents of His Expedition in 1774 (Oregon Hist. Soc. Press, 1989)
  • Published primary source: Herbert E. Bolton, Fray Juan Crespí: Missionary Explorer... 1769–1774 (UC Press, 1927)
  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California I

Sources disagree: exact Monterey dates + northern turning latitude (54°40′ vs 55°N) to confirm against Beals; NOT in registry before (Santiago rows began 1775)

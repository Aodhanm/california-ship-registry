1793 · Trinidad
port-call alta exploration arrived

RESOLVED VESSEL: the 1793 Eliza reconnaissance (Francisco de Eliza commanding overall on the Activa) surveyed the coast Juan-de-Fuca-to-San-Francisco; Juan Martínez y Zayas commanded the consort goleta Mexicana and charted Trinidad Bay (inshore work by ship's boat); his chart is reproduced in Wagner. Previously logged as "(unnamed vessel)".

  • Published primary source: Henry R. Wagner, The Cartography of the Northwest Coast of America to the Year 1800 (UC Press, 1937)
  • Published primary source: Sierra, Account of the Hezeta Expedition, ed. Wagner (CHSQ 9:3, 1930)

Sources disagree: vessel = Mexicana (well attested); whether Zayas charted Trinidad from the Mexicana or a detached ship's boat needs Wagner 1937 read directly

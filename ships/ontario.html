1822-10-13 · San Francisco
port-call alta whaler arrived

the American whaling frigate (fragata Americana ballenera) *Ontario* (from Boston/Nantucket, Capt. Alejandro [Alexander] Bunker, 21 crew, 13½ months at sea) anchored at San Francisco 1½ p.m. 13 Oct 1822; reports Spain in revolt and confusion. — Martínez to Argüello, 16 Oct 1822.

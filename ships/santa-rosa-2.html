1818-11-20 to 1818-12-15 · Monterey; Refugio; Santa Barbara; San Juan Capistrano
port-call alta warship raid

Bouchard's second ship, the corsair Santa Rosa (~300t, 18 guns), commanded by Corney: bombarded Monterey (landing at Point Pinos), then Refugio ~4-5 Dec, Santa Barbara 8-10 Dec, and a mission at lat. 33 deg 33' (San Juan Capistrano). Pairs with La Argentina

  • Published primary source: Corney, Voyages in the Northern Pacific (1896), ch. XV

Sources disagree: distinct from the Russian Santa Rosa (1823/1831) already in the registry

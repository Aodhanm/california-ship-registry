1790-09 · Monterey
port-call alta exploration arrived

Manuel Quimper's Princesa Real (the captured British sloop Princess Royal — NOT the frigate Princesa) touched Monterey Sept 1790 returning from the first detailed Spanish survey of the Strait of Juan de Fuca; reached San Blas 13 Nov 1790

  • Published primary source: Henry R. Wagner, Spanish Explorations in the Strait of Juan de Fuca (Fine Arts Press, 1933)
  • Published primary source: Navarrete, intro. to Relación... Sutil y Mexicana (Madrid, 1802)
  • Published secondary source: Bancroft, History of California I:445

Sources disagree: Bancroft loosely calls it "Princesa"; the vessel was the Princesa Real (ex-Princess Royal), a different ship
